# Froggy incline dumbbell curl — local pilot

Froggy adaptation of **Snow Rig © Blender Foundation**, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Original asset: [Snow character](https://studio.blender.org/characters/snow/v3/).
Adaptations include the Froggy appearance, exercise animation, camera, lighting,
and schematic muscle materials. The clips are silent, six seconds, 1080 square,
24 fps. Pulse, steady and clean use the same movement and camera.

The source motion and presentation were accepted by the project owner. Qualified
instructional approval remains a separate, unfinished review; this local pilot
is not a claim of professional technique certification.

Coaching copy summarizes the
[Muscle & Strength incline dumbbell curl reference](https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/incline-dumbbell-curl.html).
The app overlays accessible text and camera-projected body markers at playback
time. Highlights are schematic; pulse intensity is not measured activation.

Revision 103 adds a transparent thumbnail rendered from the accepted Froggy clean scene, frame 48, with the floor hidden. Same Snow Rig attribution and license apply. Motion files are unchanged.
