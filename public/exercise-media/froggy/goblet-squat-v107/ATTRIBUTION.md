Character appearance, equipment, authored exercise animation, educational materials and camera are modified. Original rig retained. No third-party exercise video or motion-capture data is redistributed.

These local review clips reuse accepted 800 × 720, 24 fps, six-second motion. Only available highlight modes are offered. They are not the completed 1080-square final export set. Educational muscle colours and bone-projected markers are schematic; qualified technique approval remains pending.
